NVIDIA's revenue rose from $60,922 million in fiscal 2024 (ended January 28, 2024) to $130,497 million in fiscal 2025 (ended January 26, 2025) [facts:NVDA:Revenues:FY2025], and quarterly revenue reached $57,006 million in Q3 fiscal 2026 (ended October 26, 2025), up 62% from a year ago [facts:NVDA:Revenues:Q3FY2026]. Fiscal 2025 revenue growth of 114% over fiscal 2024 compares with 126% growth in fiscal 2024 over the prior fiscal year [NVDA_10K_2025-01-26__mdna__0016] [NVDA_10K_2024-01-28__mdna__0006]. Year-over-year quarterly growth peaked at 262% in Q1 fiscal 2025 (ended April 28, 2024) and has since moderated to 69% in Q1 fiscal 2026, 56% in Q2 fiscal 2026, and 62% in Q3 fiscal 2026 [NVDA_10Q_2024-04-28__mdna__0007] [NVDA_10Q_2025-10-26__mdna__0006]. The growth driver shifted from the Hopper platform to Blackwell, which the Q3 fiscal 2026 filing says is the majority of Data Center revenue [NVDA_10K_2025-01-26__mdna__0000] [NVDA_10Q_2025-10-26__mdna__0002]. The filings flag outlook risks: H20 export licensing requirements that management says will negatively affect future revenue, and customers' availability of data centers, energy, and capital [NVDA_10Q_2025-04-27__mdna__0002] [NVDA_10Q_2025-10-26__mdna__0002]. The filings give no numeric revenue guidance.
