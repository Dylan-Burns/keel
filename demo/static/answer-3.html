Pfizer Inc (PFE)
  • Pfizer faces pricing regulation risk from the IRA, state drug affordability boards, and foreign government payors such as China's VBP process (10-K for fiscal 2024, ended December 31, 2024).
  • Regulatory approvals can be affected by differing interpretations of data, accelerated approval contingencies, and post-marketing study results, with possible label changes or loss of approval.
  • Pfizer has received government inquiries and subpoenas and has incurred significant expense and fines in some instances; its 2018 Corporate Integrity Agreement expired in May 2023 and the OIG closed the matter in January 2025.
Johnson & Johnson (JNJ)
  • The IRA subjects certain products to government-established pricing beginning in 2026, and failure to adhere to the government's interpretations pending litigation may expose J&J to penalties (10-K for fiscal 2025, ended December 28, 2025).
  • J&J says it faces significant regulatory scrutiny, and cGMP issues can lead to fines, recalls, shortages and delayed approvals.
  • The business section describes increasingly stringent regulation, including EU privacy/cyber laws and China data rules, and possible FDA civil or criminal penalties.
Merck & Co Inc (MRK)
  • Merck flags possible U.S. policy changes at HHS/FDA/CDC that could make approvals and vaccine recommendations harder, and notes FDA user fee elimination could delay inspections (10-K for fiscal 2024, ended December 31, 2024).
  • Merck lists exposure to health care reform, FCPA, data privacy, local-manufacturing mandates and sustainability rules such as the EU's CSRD.
  • Merck says it engages in public policy advocacy on cost containment and states its policies are consistent with EU directives, which it believes will not have a material effect.
Eli Lilly and Company (LLY)
  • Lilly cites risk from U.S. pricing agreements and policy efforts to align domestic prices with international benchmarks (10-K for fiscal 2025, ended December 31, 2025).
  • Evolving regulatory priorities, cGMP scrutiny, and new compliance obligations may bring investigations, penalties and increased compliance costs.
  • Lilly makes substantial capital and operating investments in company-wide quality systems and controls to maintain cGMP compliance.
AbbVie Inc (ABBV)
  • AbbVie says it cannot guarantee ongoing regulatory compliance after approval, and possible actions include recalls, production shutdowns and market withdrawals (10-K for fiscal 2024, ended December 31, 2024).
  • Biologics are subject to regulations that are often more complex and extensive than those for other pharmaceutical products.
  • AbbVie states compliance is costly and expects it to require significant technical expertise and capital investment.
