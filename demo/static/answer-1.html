Apple Inc (AAPL)
  • Operations depend on global and regional economic conditions, with sales outside the U.S. a majority of net sales and most supplier facilities outside the U.S. (fiscal 2025, ended September 27, 2025).
  • A significant majority of manufacturing is done by outsourcing partners in Asia, with single-source suppliers for many components, reducing direct control over production.
  • Apple faces intense competition, regulatory and antitrust scrutiny (including App Store investigations and the Google search licensing proceedings), and gross margin pressure.
Tesla Inc (TSLA)
  • Tesla's future depends on developing driver assistance and autonomous driving and ramping mass-market vehicles including Cybercab, with past production ramp delays (fiscal 2025, ended December 31, 2025).
  • It is highly dependent on Elon Musk, who does not devote full time to Tesla and holds other management positions.
  • Tesla faces product liability claims tied to collisions and lithium-ion battery risks, and regulations on autonomous vehicles that may delay Robotaxi expansion.
JPMorgan Chase & Co (JPM)
  • The principal risk factors summarized are legal and regulatory, political, market, and country risks, among others (fiscal 2025, ended December 31, 2025).
  • JPMorgan faces significant legal risk from civil and governmental proceedings, with exposure that could exceed established reserves.
  • Cybersecurity risks may be intensified by AI and quantum computing, and AI adoption carries competitive and workforce risks.
